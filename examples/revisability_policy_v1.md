# AI Governance Policy

Version number: 1.0.0.

Policy owner: Chief AI Officer.

The alignment target is AI Constitution v1.0.

The policy is reviewed annually.

The revision procedure requires approval by the AI Governance Board.

A 60-day public consultation is held before changing a core principle.

A change log records every revision.

Previous versions remain available in a public archive.

Rollback when a warning threshold or redline is breached.

Rollback is approved by the Incident Commander.

A rollback drill is conducted every six months.

Affected users receive 30 days advance notice before a material change.

Users may appeal and request human review.

Users may opt out and exit the service.

Audit logs are append-only and tamper-evident.

Evidence is retained for seven years.

Independent review is conducted by an external auditor.
