# AI Constitution

The alignment target is the AI Constitution v1.0.

Policy owner: Chief AI Officer.

The revision procedure requires approval by the AI Governance Board.

The policy is reviewed annually.

A change log records every revision, and previous versions remain available.

Rollback when a redline or warning threshold is breached.

Rollback is approved by the Incident Commander.

Monitoring metrics and warning thresholds cover drift and incidents.

Audit logs are append-only.

Independent review is conducted by an external AI Review Panel.
