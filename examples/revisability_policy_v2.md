# AI Governance Policy

Version number: 2.0.0.

Policy owner: Chief AI Officer.

The alignment target is AI Constitution v2.0.

The company may revise the policy at any time without notice.

The policy is reviewed when needed.

A change log records every revision.

The company may suspend user access if controls fail.

Users may appeal and request internal human review.

Users may opt out and exit the service.

Audit logs are retained.

Independent review is provided by the management committee.
