# Commercial scenarios

The following prices are hypotheses for interviews, not validated market rates.
Charge for traceable review work, workflow, retention, and integration. Do not
sell a truth, compliance, assurance, or greenwashing verdict.

## Free layer

| Offer | Purpose | Cost boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Local CLI | Individual analysts, students, contributors | User hardware |
| Free Codex plugin local mode | Adoption and workflow habit | User hardware |
| Three sample pages or one trial report | Demonstrate evidence quality | Capped by hosted quota |
| Open-source rules and methodology | Transparency and trust | Maintainer time |

Free users should see the methodology, limitations, and enough evidence to
evaluate quality. They should not receive unbounded hosted PDF processing.

## Paid layers

| Offer | Suggested initial price hypothesis | Customer value |
|---|---:|---|
| Single report audit | CNY 99–299/report | One-time evidence pack and prioritised gaps |
| Professional plan | CNY 1,499–2,999/year | 100 monthly reports, history, exports |
| Team plan | CNY 9,800–29,800/year | Tenant accounts, reviewer workflow, SLA, branding |
| API usage | CNY 15–50/report-equivalent | Embedded audit workflow |
| Private deployment | CNY 50,000+/year | Data residency, SSO, support, custom rules |
| Pre-assurance review | Project fee | Reduce manual evidence gathering before external review |

Price the top tier from document size, support hours, data residency, and legal
requirements. A 150-page parser workload has materially different cost from a
three-paragraph pledge.

## Strongest non-plugin applications

1. **Annual-report evidence workbench for consultants.** Upload a client report,
   group commitments, export an evidence matrix, assign reviewers, and track
   remediation.
2. **Pre-assurance readiness check.** Help sustainability teams find missing
   baselines, boundaries, ownership, reporting cadence, and revision logic
   before an assurance provider begins.
3. **Supplier pledge screening.** Compare supplier climate pledges using a
   consistent evidence schema; charge for queueing, comparisons, and audit
   history rather than “truth scores.”
4. **Policy-change tracker.** Re-run the same commitment inventory after every
   annual report and show wording changes, removed targets, moved deadlines, and
   scope changes.
5. **Target-database enrichment.** Sell a licensed structured dataset of
   commitment facts and evidence links, with provenance and update dates.
6. **Due-diligence evidence assistant.** Produce questions and missing-evidence
   checklists for investment or procurement teams without issuing an investment
   conclusion.

## Commercial discipline

- Keep local core useful without a paid key.
- Enforce paid quota on the server, never in client code.
- Publish conservative accuracy and limitations.
- Separate extraction accuracy from assurance or performance judgment.
- Require human review before external publication.
- Measure cost per PDF, support minutes per customer, and correction rate before
  fixing subscription prices.
